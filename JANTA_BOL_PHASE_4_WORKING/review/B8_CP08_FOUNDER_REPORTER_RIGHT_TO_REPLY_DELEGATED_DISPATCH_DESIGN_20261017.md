# JANTA BOL — B8 CP08 / Reporter-Friendly Official Questions: Founder Decision Amendment
**Date:** 17 October 2026 | **DESIGN ONLY; NO APPLICATION CODE, LIVE MAIL, DB MIGRATION, PRODUCTION WRITE OR PAID SERVICE**
**Supersedes:** CP07's FD-B8-02 proposal requiring Founder fresh AAL2 approval on EVERY official-question send.

## Founder request / intent
Founder: "Adhikaro ko sawal bhejna reporter k liye v easy kro kyuki founder sare time har jagah available nahi ho sakta."
Meaning: The platform MUST let a legitimate active **Reporter** routinely send documented, factual Right-to-Reply questions to an official/contact within their assigned reporting scope **without waiting for the Founder each time**. Founder remains sole authority to **PUBLISH/CORRECT/UNPUBLISH published News**, handle high-risk escalations and modify delegated permission settings. Sending a question is NOT publishing an Article.

## New proposed privilege boundary / delegated dispatch
- **Ordinary reporting question — delegated Reporter sends directly:** An authenticated, ACTIVE, non-suspended Reporter may create/modify an assigned-case draft and perform a *case-scoped* official-question dispatch under a one-time (or bounded) Reporter delegation issued by Owner or authorized newsroom Admin/Editor, with geography/beat/case scope and expiry. No per-question Founder OTP or AAL2 required for ordinary questions. Backend rechecks active membership, exact scope, accepted official contact channel, evidence references and safe neutral language/required fields AT SEND; front-end is never the only guard.
- **Unassigned, expired delegation or deactivated Reporter:** Draft allowed only where preexisting newsroom policy permits; **server denies dispatch**. An Editor/Admin can assign/reassign cases according to existing B1 Team permissions, without gaining Founder News publishing power. Initial staff activation/permission escalation remains protected by existing B1 Owner controls; not casually inferred from Reporter login.
- **High-risk exception:** Questions alleging criminal wrongdoing or corruption as proven, confidential-source disclosures, minors/private contact exposure, material legal threats, conflicts where JANTA BOL/Founder is the subject or sensitive personal evidence require an **Editor/Owner review** determined by an auditable risk rule; some classes may require **Founder signoff** by a later grouped decision. Reporter sees "Request review" not a fake Sent receipt. No blanket automatic gating of routine cases.
- **Protect original Founder lock:** only Founder publishes/unpublishes or changes a PUBLISHED article and makes original final Founder-only decisions. Reporter sending questions **does not** authorize article publication, editing a published headline, deletion, changing immutable evidence, final legal guilt finding, auto-starting paid Ads, or modifying audit.
- **No made-up SEND:** store separate `DRAFT`, `READY`, `SENDING`, `DISPATCH_REPORTED` (manual, unverified), `ACCEPTED_BY_CHANNEL` (provider accepted != delivered), `DELIVERY_VERIFIED`, `FAILED`, `RESPONSE_PENDING`, `PARTIAL_REPLY`, `REPLY_RECEIVED`, `NO_REPLY_AS_OF_DATE` and `LATE_REPLY` statuses as justified. Do not pretend `mailto:` launched/WhatsApp composer opened means delivery. Actual manual dispatch needs independent proof (sent email header, dispatch diary, acknowledgement or securely uploaded evidence) and credible verification method; evidence is protected, never public by default. Retain exactly what was sent, recipient reference, receipt, actor, timestamp, method, corrections and later replies. Provider failure must not erase the draft or block News.
- **Anti-abuse:** idempotent send-intent ID, double-tap guard, same-recipient cooldown, basic quotas and soft abuse review, no unknown mass mail, no external WhatsApp message without human action, non-political neutral/evidence-first wording, strict private recipient/phone/document protection.
- **Android Reporter UX goal:** On assigned Case: **(1) सवाल खोलो/बनाओ → (2) Official contact and supporting proof verify → (3) भेजो / manually sent का प्रमाण लगाओ**. Save Draft/Offline safe; never show VERIFIED-SENT offline. Every field needed for traceability must be lightweight enough for actual village-level field reporting. Editor queue handles escalations while Reporter can proceed for routine evidence-based questions.
- **Founder visibility without bottleneck:** After a normal Reporter dispatch, optional quiet B3 summary/digest to Owner/Editor; serious risk/high-risk escalation immediate restricted notification; cannot transform a notification into a fake approval. Existing B3 delivery/adapters reused.
- **Public timeline:** question wording and response are PRIVATE by default; Founder-approved redacted public record separately includes only verified facts, status and Right-to-Reply chronology; no automatic publishing.
