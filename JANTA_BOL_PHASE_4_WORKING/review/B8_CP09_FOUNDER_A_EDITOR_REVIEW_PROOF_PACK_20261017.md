## Locked business-rule intent
1. **Routine documented questions:** Active, assigned, appropriately scoped Reporter can send directly to an official using a genuine selected channel, without Founder per-send approval. No News publish/change powers gained. Dispatch/delivery state must reflect real receipts, never a pressed-button illusion.
2. **Serious or sensitive questions:** Reporter drafts, attaches available verified or clearly unverified documentary/ground references and requests **Editor review BEFORE dispatch**. Editor may approve a properly evidenced, neutral request or return it for corrections/evidence. Serious matters **also reach Founder with an auditable evidence-and-editor-decision packet**, so the Founder is informed without blocking every Reporter question. Sending is not automatically held solely for Founder reading the serious-case notice where Editor is the authorized reviewer.
3. **Exceptionally high-risk questions:** Any possible confidential-source exposure, serious privacy/minor/safety risks, JANTA BOL or Founder being the subject, material legal threat, or public disclosure needing reserved publishing authority goes to **Founder approval before the restricted action**; emergency procedures must preserve urgency and audit, not secretly bypass Founder-only publish/security locks. 4. **Founder receives a PROOF PACK, not a rumor:** Case ID + related immutable Article UUIDs (if any) + issue context; structured alleged/official-stated/verified/unknown claims; original dated documents/media refs, source verification status and hashes where available; conflict flag, material contradictory evidence; exact proposed questions and official recipient/channel; Editor review decision/reason/timestamp/actor; deadline/urgency; actual manual/provider dispatch receipts and replies **only after those events occur**. Missing proofs explicitly flagged **EVIDENCE_GAP**, never invented as present. Serious-review workflow cannot assert accusation as proven simply because Reporter wrote it.
5. **Privacy:** Pack links restricted to existing authorized roles/Owner MFA; Founder sees full authorized verified material, but low-noise notifications expose **case ID, priority, review status, safe short summary**, not confidential sources, personal contacts, bank records or full exhibits. No automatic public post; Founder controls any approved public redacted chronology and publishing.
6. **Status and offline safety:** `DRAFT`, `EDITOR_REVIEW_PENDING`, `EVIDENCE_GAP`, `EDITOR_APPROVED`/`RETURNED`, `FOUNDER_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `DISPATCH_ATTEMPTED`, `CHANNEL_ACCEPTED`, `DELIVERY_VERIFIED`, `FAILED`, `PARTIAL_REPLY`, `LATE_REPLY` remain separate auditable states, with real proof associated with each transition. No false `SENT` merely from WhatsApp/email composer opening; network/offline means not yet sent.
7. **Role boundary:** Reporter collection/ordinary send; Editor serious evidence-review; Founder reserved high-risk decisions, publication/correction/unpublish and sensitive evidence authority. No duplicate Article engine; existing B1 roles, B2 immutable audit, B3 notification, private editorial storage and E5 evidence reused. No new paid provider or automatic mass sending.
